# Session 4 Consolidate

Use this session to consolidate your knowledge and write up what you have done so far.

Short History of Computing: In studying the history of computing, i experienced how technology evolved from simple mechanical tools to the powerful digital systems we use today. I began by learning about early devices such as the abacus and mechanical calculators, which showed how humans first approached problem-solving and computation. I then examined the contributions of key pioneers. The important parf of my learning was understanding how innovations such as the transistor, integrated circuits, and microprocessors transformed computers by making them smaller, faster and more efficient.  


Computer Architecture: I gained a clear understanding of how a computer's internal components work together to process information. I started by learning about the basic structure of a computer system, including the CPU, memory, input/output devices, and storage. I explored how the CPU executes instructions through trhe fetch-decode-executive cycle, which helped me understand how programs actually run at the hardware level. Another part of my learning involved studying instruction sets and how they define the operations a processor can perform. I examined how pipelining improves CPU efficiency by executing multiple instructions at once. I also explored how buses, I/O controllers, and system architecture connect all components together.
