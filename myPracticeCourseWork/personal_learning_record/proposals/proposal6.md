# Council Proposal 6

## Explanation of this proposal
*What do you think this proposal might mean? How might it be implemented?*

Making the council's website and app the primary gateways for the public to engage with council services means that these platforms are designed to be the main point of access for residents to interact with council services. This approach allows for a seamless and efficient experience, where residents can easily find information, apply for services, and communicate with council staff. The digital-first strategy aims to enhance the overall service delivery by providing a user-friendly interface that meets the needs of the public. Most services are designed so that residents start on the website or app rather than phoning, visiting in person, or sending paper forms, e.g, reporting issues, paying bills, booking services, applying for permits, accessing information.

A digital-first approach can be implemented by redesigning council services so the website and council app become the main ways residents access information and complete tasks. This begins with mapping all customers journeys and conerting the most common interactions such as reporting issues, paying bills, booking services and submitting application into streamlined, end-to-end digital processes. The website and app should offer clear navigation, secure accounts, real-time updates and the ability to track requests. Internal systems must be integrated so staff can process digital submissions efficiently and consistently.

To drive uptake, the council should promote digital channels as the default option in communications, while still providing assisted-digital support for those who need help. Staff training, service-design standards and ongoing performance monitoring ensure services remain accessible, user-friendly and reliable. Acessibility compliance, mobile optimisation and strong data-security measures are essential. Feedback tools within the website and app help identify issues and guide continuous improvement. By prioritising convenient self-service options and ensuring digital channels are quicker and easier than traditional routes, the council can reduce demand on face-to-face and phone services, improve customer experience and deliver services more efficiently.
